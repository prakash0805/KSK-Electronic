About KSK Electronics

Madurai's trusted name for TV repair, since 2013

KS
K. Shiva Kumar Owner & Service Technician

KSK Electronics has been repairing televisions in K Pudhur, Madurai for over 13 years, earning a 4.8-star rating across 17 customer reviews. What started as a small neighbourhood repair counter has grown into a full-service TV repair shop trusted across greater Madurai — without losing the personal, straight-talking approach that built it.

We specialise in Philips TV repair alongside every other major brand — Plasma, LCD, LED, HDTV, and rear-projection sets. Whether it's a minor glitch or a major chip-level fault, our technicians diagnose the real problem before recommending a fix, and explain it in plain terms before any work starts.
